# Device A Maintenance Manual
## Startup troubleshooting
WARNING: Disconnect and isolate power before inspecting electrical components.

1. Repetitive clicking during startup may indicate worn relay contacts.
2. With power isolated, inspect relay contacts and connections.
3. Check the relay coil supply according to the manufacturer's procedure.

A defective relay is a possible cause, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Overheating
Inspect cooling fans, blocked air intakes, and ventilation clearances.
